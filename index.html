Meet the machines

Gas turbines burn fuel to spin a shaft. Wind turbines catch moving air. Both turn motion into electricity.

About this project

TByieldo comes from a machine learning project for the power grid. The goal: predict gas turbine energy output with regression models, check them carefully and improve them with ensemble learning.

Regression modelling

Models that estimate a number (power in MW) from inputs.

Feature engineering

Choosing and cleaning the right inputs before training.

Evaluation

Measured with standard regression metrics such as RMSE and R².

Ensembles and reliability

Combining models, validating, studying errors and monitoring over time.

Note: this demo runs a simple linear formula in your browser (the classic fit for the Combined Cycle Power Plant data), so it works offline and loads fast. Plug in the trained ensemble model for production numbers.
